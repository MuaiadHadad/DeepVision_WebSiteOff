BioVision™ by DeepVision

Hyperspectral imaging + AI for faster, smarter wound care

Detect bacterial load, map tissue oxygenation and hydration, and pinpoint areas to debride — in one portable device designed for clinics, wards, and home care.

We are DeepVision Medical AI

DeepVision builds decision-support tools that bring laboratory-grade insight to the point of care. Our flagship device, BioVision, combines hyperspectral imaging with AI to support clinicians in diagnosing and managing chronic wounds more quickly and consistently.

  • Portable Class I medical device under CE roadmap.
  • Integrated metrics: StO₂, oxy/deoxyhemoglobin, tissue hydration (H₂O).
  • Real-time bacterial fluorescence detection and AI-guided debridement support.

From pixels to physiology

BioVision captures hundreds of wavelengths, then translates them into clinically relevant maps — oxygenation, hemoglobin, hydration — while highlighting suspected bacterial fluorescence.

Bacterial fluorescence

Visualize suspected bacterial load in real time to support timely intervention.

Tissue oxygenation (StO₂)

Map superficial oxygenation to distinguish perfusion issues from infection trends.

Hydration mapping

Assess local water content (H₂O) to inform dressing strategy and edema management.

Oxy / deoxyhemoglobin

See relative oxy/deoxy Hb distribution to monitor tissue status around the wound bed.

AI decision support

Highlight areas likely requiring debridement, based on annotated clinical datasets.

Secure cloud

Optional cloud workflows for audit trails, longitudinal tracking, and team collaboration.

Portable & ergonomic

Bedside-friendly design for clinics, wards, community nursing and home visits.

3D wound topography

Depth-aware imaging (RGB‑D) to support documentation and healing assessment.

Skin‑tone robust

Spectral approach designed to deliver consistent results across diverse skin tones.
